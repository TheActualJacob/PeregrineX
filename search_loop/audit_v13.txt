merely crash-finding -- the certification-relevant claim.

phys ratio = same, collapsing the inert u5 coordinate when u4<0.5
A large raw-minus-phys gap would mean the win is partly inert-dim
farming; a small gap means the discoveries are physically distinct.
Per-proxy spread shows whether the win is proxy-specific.
